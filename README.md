# 💡 What is WYD?

WYD is an AI-powered social layer for conferences.

Share only the parts of your schedule you want, discover where your friends are going, and receive personalized recommendations about where your group should go next.

Instead of opening five different apps, simply ask:

> **"wyd?"**

# Why Blockchain?

Calendars, locations, and social graphs are some of the most personal data we have.

Instead of trusting a centralized platform with that information, WYD uses blockchain to give users portable identity and verifiable access permissions.

Private data stays encrypted.

Permissions stay under the user's control.

---

# 🟣 Built with Sui

WYD uses the Sui ecosystem to make privacy programmable.

### Walrus

Encrypted storage for calendars and shared event data.

### Seal

Programmable access control for who can view your schedule.

### zkLogin

Instant onboarding with familiar Web2 authentication.

No wallet setup required.

---

# 🌎 Built with World

Every AI concierge is backed by a verified human.

Using World AgentKit prevents:

- Fake attendees
- Spam invitations
- Bot accounts
- Sybil attacks
- Fake check-ins

This creates trusted social recommendations without sacrificing privacy.

---

# 📈 Built with The Graph

The Graph powers our recommendation engine by indexing conference and blockchain activity.

Our AI can answer questions like:

- Which sponsor booth is trending?
- Which side events are gaining momentum?
- Which communities are gathering nearby?
- Where are builders with similar interests meeting?

Structured data leads to smarter recommendations.
